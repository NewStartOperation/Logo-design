The Bella Surf Program

From first wave to confident surfer

Our programs are built around you. On day one your coach checks your level, then each 2 hour lesson moves you through four stages, with video analysis and photos so you can see your progress.

1

Foundations

Getting comfortable and safe in the ocean.

  • Ocean safety, currents and reading the beach
  • Paddling and board control
  • Pop up on the sand, then in the white water
Standing up and riding white water
2

Green Waves

Moving out past the white water.

  • Timing and catching unbroken waves
  • Angling left and right along the wave
  • Surf etiquette and right of way
Riding green waves
3

Progression

Becoming an independent surfer.

  • Turning, trimming and building speed
  • Choosing the right waves
  • Positioning in the lineup
Surfing on your own
4

Performance

Taking your surfing further.

  • Bottom and top turns
  • Video analysis of your surfing
  • Preparing for reef breaks and surf trips

Good to know

Frequently asked questions

I have never surfed before. Can I join?

Absolutely. Our private lessons, group lessons and the 5 Lesson Program are made for complete beginners. Kuta Beach has gentle, sandy bottom waves that are perfect for learning.

How long is a lesson?

Every lesson is 2 hours. Lessons run throughout the day, and your coach picks the best time based on the tide.

What is included?

A surfboard, surf photos of your session and drinking water. Program lessons also include video analysis.

What should I bring?

Swimwear, reef safe sunscreen and a towel. A rashguard or surf shirt is a good idea to protect you from the sun.

Is there a minimum age?

Kids can learn from age 6. Family lessons are very welcome.

Do you offer hotel pickup?

Yes, hotel pickup is available for an extra fee. Just let us know when you book.

What is your cancellation policy?

Please give us at least 12 to 24 hours notice if you need to cancel. We are always flexible for genuine reasons such as illness or bad weather.

What happens if the waves or weather are bad?

Your safety comes first. If conditions turn, we take a break and go back out when it is safe. If it stays unsafe, we will shorten the lesson.

How do I pay?

Send a booking request and we will confirm on WhatsApp. Payment is by bank transfer or card.
