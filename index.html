Concept · The Forward A

Bold enough to stand out. Simple enough to scale.

An “A” built from two warm gradient strokes, with a bright crossbar that breaks out of the letter and pushes forward — momentum, ignition and progress in a single, clean gesture.

ardent
01 — Idea

How the mark works

Three colours, three strokes, one idea. Each colour owns a part of the symbol, and where they meet they overlap instead of blending into mud — so it stays crisp from a billboard down to a 16 px favicon.

Red — the foundation

The left stroke rises from deep red: energy, confidence and the base the brand stands on.

Orange — the overlap

The right stroke laps over the apex, creating a subtle fold where the two colours meet — a clever overlay, not a blur.

Yellow — the spark

The crossbar cuts through the letter and extends past it: the brand moving ahead. It also reads as a progress bar — a nod to tech.

03 — Small sizes

Built to survive tiny

Thick, rounded strokes and a single open gap keep the “A” recognisable at favicon and app-icon sizes — no hairlines or fine detail to disappear.

04 — Colour

Colour guidelines

Four warm tones plus a near-black ink. The gradient always runs red → yellow (left-to-right or bottom-to-top) so the energy reads as rising and moving forward.

05 — Spacing

Clear space & minimum size

“X” equals the height of the crossbar. Keep at least 1X of empty space around the logo on every side so it never feels crowded.

Rules of thumb

  • Clear space: minimum 1X on all sides (X = crossbar height).
  • Minimum size: icon 16 px / 5 mm; full lockup 80 px wide / 22 mm.
  • Use full colour on white, paper or ink backgrounds.
  • On photos or the gradient, switch to the white monochrome version.
  • Single-colour print (stamps, embroidery, fax): solid black or white.
  • Typeface: Sora SemiBold for the wordmark and headings; Inter for body text.
